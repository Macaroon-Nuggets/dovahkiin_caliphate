OVERVIEW
The Dovahkiin Caliphate is a juvenocratic and theocratic constitutional monarchy. The Sublime Porte refers to its federal government. The Head of State is Caliph Dovahkiin, who de jure rules in perpetuity. Since they have no last name, the Caliphate takes on their first name. Dovahkiin is most often referred to by their epithet, “The New Kid.” In the current era of fragile peace, Dovahkiin has retired from politics, leaving the democratic institutions de jure in complete control of the state. The Caliph serves as the temporal leader of the religion of Parkerism, whilst Pope Snowball serves as Parkerists spiritual leader. South Parkers highly value democracy, liberty, justice, equality, acceptance, compassion, rationality, and the unification of light and dark. The Parkerist religion was only recently created in 2025 and is based on measurable fact. God, different planes of existence, and supernatural beings have all been observed and proven to exist during the Great Suffering, a tumultuous period of time from 1997-2025. As of 2026, the SPDC fully occupies five inhabited worlds as core territory, Earth, Io, Fognl, Marklar, and Kokujon and is locked in cold war with the Federation of Planets. The native governments of Fognl and Kokujon are members of the FoP and are currently governments-in-exile. The SPDC also occupies a small part of the planet Dawnbreak as the Viceroyalty of New Zaron.
OUT OF CHARACTER (OOC)
This nation is inspired by South Park and was originally created before season 27, but later accommodated many aspects of seasons 27 and 28 as they would fit my lore very well. I first came up with the idea for the Parkerist religion while playing the After the End total conversion mod for Crusader Kings III. I first created a custom faith as a heresy of Mormonism based on the idea that some people in a post-apocalyptic America believed that all the events of South Park really happened and made a religion out of it. The name Parkerism comes from both Trey Parker and the demonym of South Park, South Parker. I would later make Parkerism into its own mod in 2025. In 2024, I was working on a Legend of Zelda, Death Note, and South Park crossover fanfiction, parts of which would inspire an unreleased solely South Park fic called “Cartmanreich: All Hell Breaks Loose.” It would involve the player character from the canon South Park games, Dovahkiin “the New Kid,” leading a revolution against the United States. I had reimagined them as an amalgamation of every South Park fan. I made them racially ambiguous and have Dissociative Identity Disorder, previously known as Multiple Personality Disorder, with each fan being a soul within them. I decided to merge Cartmanreich with Parkerism by having the Freedom Pals create it during the revolution. I would later have the realization that Dovahkiin should be a Caliph to go with Snowball. In CK3, the Caliph is a temporal head of faith, while the Pope is a spiritual head of faith, so I thought to put them together in Parkerism. Muhammad, along with the rest of the Super Best Friends, would grant Dovahkiin the title of Caliph since their immense wisdom from being made up of millions of different people that all had meta knowledge of their universe made them the perfect person to free Earth from the existential threat of Nerzod Productions.
