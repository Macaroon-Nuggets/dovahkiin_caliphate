SUMMARY
The Dovahkiin Caliphate (SPDC) was founded during the American Warlord Period on the Thirtieth of May in the Two-thousandth and Twenty-fifth day of our Lord (30/5/2025), when Muhammad of the Super Best Friends officially appointed King Dovahkiin of Colorado as the Caliph of Parkerism. The Caliphate would fulfil the prophecy, open the gates of Hell, peacefully integrate Canada and Mexico, and restore American democracy. The creation of Parkerism and the Super Best Friends (SBF) backing the Caliphate triggered political upheaval and civil war across Earth, leading to the Third World War, which resulted in Terran unification under the SPDC. From there, Dovahkiin was able to shatter the Federation of Planets’ (FoP) barrier around the Earth-Moon system and achieve independence from Nerzod Productions.
